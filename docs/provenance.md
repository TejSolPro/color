# Data provenance policy

Color does not treat online availability as permission to redistribute data.

Every source package must record:

- the source title, creator, maintainer, and canonical URL;
- the exact upstream version or retrieval date;
- the applicable license and attribution requirements;
- integrity hashes for imported artifacts;
- the conversion or transcription method;
- known uncertainty and corrections;
- which fields originated upstream and which were generated locally.

Sources without a clear redistribution basis must not be committed. Scans, editorial descriptions, and modern reproductions require separate analysis even when the underlying colors or historical facts may not be protected.

Generated ramps and semantic tokens must retain ancestry back to their input colors. Transformations should record both their operation and perceptual distance.

The first historical corpus will not be published until its source and redistribution terms have been documented in a source ledger.
